# Blu-H Volunteer Terms & Conditions

## 1. About Blu-H  
Blu-H is a not-for-profit initiative focused on developing and deploying data-driven solutions for flood risk and resilience. Volunteers support our mission by contributing skills, time, and expertise.

## 2. Volunteer Role  
By registering as a volunteer, you acknowledge that:
- Your participation is voluntary and unpaid  
- You may contribute time, expertise, data, or other support  
- There is no guarantee of work, assignments, or ongoing engagement  

Blu-H reserves the right to accept, decline, or discontinue volunteer participation at its discretion.

## 3. Code of Conduct  
Volunteers agree to:
- Act professionally, ethically, and respectfully  
- Collaborate in good faith with the Blu-H team and partners  
- Avoid any activity that could harm Blu-H, its partners, or beneficiaries  

Blu-H reserves the right to suspend or terminate volunteer participation at any time.

## 4. Data Protection & Privacy (GDPR)  
Blu-H processes personal data in accordance with applicable data protection laws, including the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR).

By submitting this form, you consent to Blu-H collecting and processing your personal data for:
- Managing volunteer applications and coordination  
- Communication related to Blu-H activities  

Your data will:
- Be stored securely and access-controlled  
- Not be shared with third parties without your consent, unless required by law or for operational purposes  
- Be retained only as long as necessary for the purposes described  

You have the right to:
- Access your data  
- Request correction or deletion  
- Withdraw consent at any time  

Requests can be made via the contact details below.

## 6. Confidentiality  
Volunteers may have access to sensitive or unpublished information. You agree to:
- Keep confidential information private  
- Not share data, models, or materials without authorization
